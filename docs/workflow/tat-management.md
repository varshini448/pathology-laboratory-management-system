# TAT Management

## Pathology Intelligence Platform

Turnaround Time (TAT) management tracks the time required to process pathology cases and identifies cases that may exceed their expected processing targets.

---

## 1. Priority Targets

| Priority | Target TAT |
|----------|------------|
| NORMAL   | 72 hours   |
| URGENT   | 48 hours   |
| STAT     | 24 hours   |

---

## 2. TAT Process

```text
Case Created
     ↓
Workflow Events Recorded
     ↓
Stage Start / Completion
     ↓
Duration Calculation
     ↓
Case TAT Evaluation
     ↓
Target Comparison
     ↓
Within Target / Delayed
---

## 3. TAT Tracking

The system can use workflow timestamps to calculate:

- Stage duration
- Case processing duration
- Total turnaround time
- Target compliance
- Delay status

This allows laboratory personnel to identify cases requiring attention.

---

## 4. Operational Intelligence

TAT information can support:

- Delayed case identification
- Workflow bottleneck analysis
- Workload monitoring
- Priority management
- Laboratory performance analysis

---

## 5. Future AI Integration

TAT data provides a foundation for:

- TAT prediction
- Delay risk prediction
- Bottleneck detection
- Priority recommendations
- Workload forecasting

AI outputs are intended to support authorized users rather than replace laboratory decision-making.
